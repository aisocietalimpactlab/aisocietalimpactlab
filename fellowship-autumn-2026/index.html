Fellowship enquiries
01

Curriculum

The Fellowship is designed to bring out emerging voices working on socio-political implications of emerging AI technology. It will be run remotely for approx. 8-10 weeks starting in late September 2026. We anticipate it will require four hours of time commitment per week: one hour on a weekly call and three hours of individual work. The concrete dates and programme details will be shared with shortlisted candidates.

02

Eligibility

The programme is targeting recent graduates in social sciences, humanities, politics and international relations, as well as early career researchers. However, we encourage diversity of both thought and representation, and would be happy to receive applications from other backgrounds (STEM, arts, etc.). Whilst previous experience on the subject of AI is welcome, it is not required.

03

Benefits

Selected candidates will be part of the exciting mission to build a community of individuals that are not indifferent to the challenges posed by rapid technological advancement, receive mentorship from one of the fellows at the AI Societal Impact Lab, work on a group project, create their own analytical piece to be published on the the AI Societal Impact Lab website, and take part in dedicated workshops and seminars. 

2026 APPLICATIONS

Applications are now closed.

Applications were reviewed on a rolling basis until the programme was filled or 4 September 2026, whichever came first. Spaces were strictly limited, and early applications were encouraged.

Programme dates and details will be shared with shortlisted candidates.
