Privacy Policy

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit this website or email us.

Last updated 28 September 2026

Who we are

AI Societal Impact Lab (AISIL) is responsible for the personal information it receives when you contact us through this website. You can reach us at info@aisocietalimpactlab.com.

Information we handle

If you email us, we receive your email address, name if you provide one, your message and any information you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

We do not offer accounts or web forms on this site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is disabled. The site’s own code does not include analytics or advertising trackers, set cookies, or store information in your browser.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you visit, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address, request details and security data to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare may set essential cookies when its security features require them. Cloudflare describes its handling of visitors to customer sites in its privacy policy.

Why we use your information

We use messages to respond to enquiries and manage the correspondence that follows. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in communicating about the Lab’s work and responding to people who contact us. Where we must keep or disclose information to comply with a legal obligation, that obligation is our lawful basis.

You are not required to email us, but we cannot respond to an enquiry without the contact details and information needed to understand it. We do not use information from this site for solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Who receives it and where

Cloudflare processes visit data to deliver and secure the site. Our email provider handles messages sent to us. We may share a message with the relevant AISIL team member so they can respond, and disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell contact information.

Cloudflare and email services may process information outside the UK. Cloudflare describes its international transfer safeguards in its GDPR information. If you want details about the services used for a particular message, email us.

Links to LinkedIn, X and other external sites take you to those services only when you choose to follow them. Their privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, manage any continuing relationship and meet legal requirements, then delete it when no longer needed. Cloudflare controls the retention of its own service data; see its privacy policy for details.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal information, or object to its use. You may also have a right to data portability. You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests at any time. Email info@aisocietalimpactlab.com to make a request.

If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when our website or information practices change. The date above shows when this notice was last updated.
